Academy Manufactor…) → converti in valore (Frodo pesca, Haliya, Tireless Provisioner)
→ drena (Cauldron Familiar loop, Marionette Apprentice, Mirkwood Bats, Vito). Delney
raddoppia quasi tutti i trigger (forza ≤2). Displaced Dinosaurs = finisher (ogni Food
storico entra 7/7). Combo confermata: Warren Soultrader + Cauldron Familiar +
(Academy Manufactor / Peregrin Took).
